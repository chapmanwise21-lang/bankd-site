How It Works

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

🔒 The short version: Bankd collects absolutely no data. Everything stays on your device. We have no servers, no accounts, and no way to access your family's information — because we never receive it.

Overview

Bankd ("the App") is a family screen time management application developed by Chapman Wise. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in connection with your use of the App.

The core principle is simple: we collect nothing. Bankd operates entirely on your device. No data is transmitted to any server, cloud service, or third party.

Information We Do Not Collect

Bankd does not collect, store, transmit, or process any of the following:

  • Personal information (names, email addresses, phone numbers)
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or app behavior data
  • Health or fitness data (HealthKit data is read locally and never transmitted)
  • Financial information
  • Children's information of any kind
  • Crash reports or diagnostic data

Data Storage

All data created within Bankd — including user profiles, token balances, activity history, chore records, and screen time requests — is stored exclusively on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework.

This data is not synced to iCloud, not backed up to any external service, and is not accessible to the developer or any third party. If you delete the app, all data is permanently removed from your device.

HealthKit

Bankd may request access to Apple HealthKit to read step count, workout, sleep, and activity data for the purpose of awarding tokens. This data is:

  • Read locally on your device only
  • Never transmitted, stored externally, or shared
  • Used solely to calculate token awards within the app
  • Entirely optional — the app functions without HealthKit access

HealthKit permissions can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Bankd.

Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Bankd is designed for use by families including children under 13. Because we collect no data whatsoever, we are compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by design.

We do not collect, use, or disclose personal information from children under 13. No account creation is required. No internet connection is required to use the app.

Third-Party Services

Bankd does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or data collection services. There are no SDKs, trackers, or external dependencies that collect user data.

Data Export & Deletion

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • Export: Navigate to Settings → Privacy & Security → Export Data to export a copy of your family's data as a JSON file via iOS share sheet
  • Delete: Navigate to Settings → Privacy & Security → Wipe All Data to permanently delete all app data from your device
  • Uninstall: Deleting the app removes all associated data permanently

Security

Bankd includes built-in security features to protect your family's information on-device:

  • PIN lock — required to access parent and guardian views
  • Privacy screen — app content is hidden when backgrounded
  • Configurable lock timeout — PIN required after customizable idle period

Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and note the change in the App Store release notes. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
