Bankd Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 8, 2026

Data Not Collected

Overview

Bankd is a family screen time management app designed for parents and kids. This Privacy Policy explains how Bankd handles information. The short version: Bankd does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Data We Collect

We Collect Nothing

Bankd does not collect any data. Specifically:

  • No account creation or login is required
  • No data is transmitted to any server
  • No analytics or crash reporting services are used
  • No advertising SDKs or third-party tracking is included
  • No usage data, identifiers, or diagnostics are collected

All information you enter into Bankd — family member names, chore lists, screen time settings, token balances, activity logs — is stored exclusively on your device using Apple's on-device SwiftData framework. This data never leaves your device.

HealthKit

Bankd may request access to Apple HealthKit to allow kids to earn screen time tokens by meeting health-related goals (steps, active minutes, etc.). If you grant this permission:

  • HealthKit data is read on-device only to calculate earned tokens
  • No HealthKit data is stored, transmitted, or shared
  • HealthKit data is never used for advertising
  • You can revoke HealthKit access at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Health

Bankd's use of HealthKit complies with Apple's HealthKit guidelines. We do not use HealthKit data for any purpose other than the in-app token earning feature.

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Bankd is designed to be used by families, including children under 13. Because Bankd collects no data whatsoever, the app is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

  • We do not collect personal information from children or adults
  • We do not require any account creation
  • We have no server-side storage of any user data
  • Parents maintain complete control over all app data on their device

Parents and guardians are in full control of the app through the parent PIN system. No child data is accessible to Bankd or any third party.

Push Notifications

Bankd uses Apple's on-device notification system (UserNotifications framework) to deliver local notifications — for example, notifying a parent when a child submits a screen time or chore request. These notifications are:

  • Generated and delivered entirely on-device
  • Not routed through any remote notification server
  • Not used to send marketing or promotional messages

You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings > Notifications > Bankd.

Data Storage and Security

All Bankd data is stored on your device using Apple SwiftData. We recommend keeping your device protected with a passcode or Face ID. Bankd also includes:

  • A parent PIN lock to prevent unauthorized access within the app
  • An auto-lock feature that prompts for PIN after a configurable idle period
  • A privacy screen that obscures content when the app is backgrounded
  • A full data wipe option in Settings if you want to remove all app data

Your Data Rights

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you have full and direct control at all times. Within the app, you can:

  • Export all your data as a plain-text file via Settings > Privacy > Export My Data
  • Delete all data permanently via Settings > Privacy > Delete All Data
  • Uninstall the app to remove all data (iOS deletes SwiftData on uninstall)

There is no account to close, no server-side data to request deletion of, and no data retention period — because we hold no data.

Third-Party Services

Bankd does not integrate any third-party services, SDKs, or frameworks other than Apple's own system frameworks (SwiftUI, SwiftData, HealthKit, UserNotifications). There are no:

  • Analytics services (e.g., Firebase, Mixpanel, Amplitude)
  • Advertising networks
  • Crash reporting tools
  • Social login or OAuth providers
  • Cloud sync providers

Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will revise the Effective Date at the top and post the updated policy at this URL. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy. We will never introduce data collection without clear notice.
